Case study · Enterprise platform

GenAI Workspace

An internal ChatGPT-style assistant available to every e& employee, giving staff a secure alternative to public AI tools. Company data stays inside the company's environment.

Role
AI Engineer Intern
When
2026, ongoing
Scope
Backend, UI and Studio features
Status
Internal platform

The problem

Teams wanted modern AI assistants, but public tools could not see confidential documents and gave the company no control over which models were used, by whom, or at what cost. The platform had to feel as good as a consumer chat product while running behind corporate sign-in, with no company data leaving the network.

My contribution

  • Azure OpenAI integration. Connected large language models through REST APIs to power chat, content generation, summarisation and question answering.
  • Prompt engineering. Designed and tested prompt templates that made answers more accurate, relevant and consistent for business users.
  • Secure data handling. Kept sensitive company data inside e&'s Azure environment across the features I worked on.
  • Shipping with the team. Tested, debugged, documented and deployed new platform features with senior engineers.

What the platform does

  • Streamed chat with history and projects. Projects group chats with reference documents whose content is used in every project conversation.
  • Governed model access. Admins choose which models are available and set usage limits, so the company controls cost and access.
  • Tool skills. The assistant can plan and run approved tools such as web search, document authoring with Word/PDF/Excel export, image generation and handing a chat off to Presentation Studio. Each step is validated and permission-checked before it runs.
  • Enterprise controls: single sign-on, role-based access, per-team quotas, feedback capture and reporting.

The hard parts

Most of the difficulty was in correctness under real conditions: long-running AI operations that can be cancelled or interrupted, streaming responses that must never land in the wrong chat, and fair usage limits across many teams at once.

The other challenge was making a multi-service system easy to run, so a new developer can get a working stack from one configuration file and one launcher command.

Case study · Generative product

AI Presentation Studio

Turns a short brief or a stack of source documents into a reviewed outline, then a fully editable deck, then a native PowerPoint or PDF, in English, Arabic or both.

Role
AI Engineer Intern
When
Jul – Oct 2026
Built on
Open-source Presenton (Apache 2.0)
Status
Integrated into Workspace

The problem

A language model can write slide text, but it cannot reliably produce a correct, on-brand PowerPoint file. Employees spend hours turning reports into decks, and generated decks are useless if they arrive as flat images that nobody can edit.

Approach

  • Models write structure, code writes files. The model produces a validated plan and slide content; deterministic services lay out the slides and build the file. Images are used for illustrations, never for whole slides.
  • Human in the loop. Users review and edit the outline before the full deck is generated, which saves the most expensive step from being wasted.
  • Source-grounded. PDF, Word, Excel, Markdown and existing PowerPoint files are parsed and used as evidence for the content.
  • Brand-ready. Standard and Etisalat-branded decks share one generation path, with right-to-left support for Arabic and mixed layouts.
  • Export pipeline. A pinned Node and Chromium runtime renders each deck to PPTX and PDF in a separate process.

From prototype to product

The project started as a standalone prototype, followed by a ten-part engineering specification covering requirements, architecture, the AI pipeline, data model, API contracts, template engineering, security, operations, testing and the delivery plan. The final version moved inside GenAI Workspace: it shares its sign-in, model catalog, quotas and one-operation-per-account rule. Generation has a hard 420-second ceiling, and interrupted decks keep enough state to be recovered.

Case study · Interactive kiosk

Package Advisor

A touchless retail kiosk. A shopper stands in front of a webcam, answers a few questions with hand gestures, and leaves with the best-fit Etisalat mobile or home plan as a QR code.

Role
AI Engineer Intern
When
Aug – Oct 2026
Runs on
Chrome, no server or build step
Languages
English and العربية

The problem

Shoppers in a store rarely know which of twenty plans suits them, and staff are not always free. A touchscreen kiosk is unhygienic and easy to ignore. The goal was something that invites people to walk up and play with it, and still gives a useful answer in under a minute.

What it does

  • Real-time hand tracking in the browser. MediaPipe Hands supplies 21 landmarks per hand; p5.js draws the whole experience on a canvas.
  • An 11-screen state machine with calibration, a language choice, a short gesture tutorial, the quiz, an animated "building your shortlist" screen and the result with a QR code.
  • A recommendation engine that scores 20 real mobile and home plans against answers about data, minutes, speed, roaming, TV and contract length.
  • Full bilingual narration. UI strings come from one dictionary, and voice lines are swapped to Arabic recordings automatically by filename.
  • Either hand works. Whichever hand completes calibration drives the session; the other hand gives a thumbs-up to restart.

The hard parts

Flat picture coordinates break when fingers point at the camera or the hand turns. The fix was to move handshape checks (open hand, fist, thumbs up or down) to MediaPipe's real-world 3D coordinates in metres, lightly smoothed, while swipes and hovering still use screen position. A debug panel (press H) shows both views and each gesture's verdict side by side.

Stores are crowded. The app ignores small, distant hands and always follows the person closest to the kiosk. The cut-off is calibrated on site by reading the hand size of a user and of a passer-by from the panel and choosing a value between them.
